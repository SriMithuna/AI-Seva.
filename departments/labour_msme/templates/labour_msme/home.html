Common AI Seva processing

  1. Citizen selects a scheme or service and prepares a guidance request.
  2. AI Seva records the request and assigns a tracking ID.
  3. The seven SLPS factors are scored and a priority band is assigned.
  4. Verified official deadlines and officer-confirmed Critical cases guide the officer queue.
  5. The officer reviews the record, adds remarks and updates its status.
  6. The citizen tracks the AI Seva guidance record; official application status remains on the relevant government portal.

If the PMKVY scheme and training-registration routes describe the same open course request for the same learner, AI Seva reuses the existing record instead of creating another score.
